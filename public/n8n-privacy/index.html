1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Mocambo Ltda. (“we”, “us”, or “Mocambo”) handles information in connection with Automador de mensagens - Mocambo (the “Application”). The Application is an internal-only automation used by Mocambo staff to send and manage operational messages through workflows running on n8n, with optional authorization to Google accounts via Google OAuth.

The Application is not offered as a public consumer product. It is not intended for use by the general public or by unrelated third-party end users.

2. Who this applies to

This policy applies to:

  • Mocambo Ltda. administrators and authorized staff who connect a Google account to the Application; and
  • any personal data that may appear inside messages or related Google resources processed solely for Mocambo’s internal business operations (for example, event inquiries or customer communications handled by our team).

3. Google user data we access

When an authorized Mocambo administrator grants access through Google OAuth, the Application may access Google user data and Google APIs that the administrator explicitly authorizes for message automation. Depending on the permissions configured in our Google Cloud OAuth consent screen, this may include, for example:

  • Gmail — read, create, send, draft, label, or otherwise manage email messages needed to automate communications;
  • Google Sheets / Drive / related Workspace APIs — read or write operational data used by the automation (templates, lists, logs, or files referenced by workflows), only if those scopes are enabled;
  • basic account identifiers required by OAuth (such as email address of the connected account) to authenticate and maintain the connection.

The Application only uses Google user data for the features that Mocambo staff have configured in n8n. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI/ML models.

4. How we use Google user data

Google user data accessed by the Application is used exclusively to:

  • authenticate authorized Mocambo accounts via Google OAuth;
  • run internal n8n workflows that automate sending, receiving, or organizing messages related to Mocambo’s business operations; and
  • maintain reliable operation of those workflows (including error handling and operational logs within our automation environment).

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data storage and sharing

  • Storage: OAuth tokens and workflow-related data may be stored in our n8n automation environment and related infrastructure controlled by Mocambo Ltda., solely to keep the automations running.
  • Retention: We retain Google user data and tokens only for as long as needed to operate the Application or until access is revoked. When a connection is disconnected or access is revoked, we delete or invalidate stored tokens and stop further access.
  • Sharing: We do not sell or rent Google user data. We do not share Google user data with third parties except as needed to operate the automation infrastructure itself (for example, hosting the n8n instance), to comply with law, or with service providers acting on our instructions under appropriate safeguards.
  • Access: Access is limited to authorized Mocambo Ltda. personnel who need it to maintain the Application.

6. Data we do not collect from the public

This Privacy Policy page itself does not require login and does not collect personal information from visitors beyond ordinary web server logs that may be generated by our website host. The Application does not provide a public sign-up experience for external users.

7. Your choices and rights

Authorized Google account holders can:

  • revoke the Application’s access at any time in their Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions;
  • request deletion of stored tokens or related automation data by emailing espacomocambo@gmail.com.

If you believe personal data about you was processed through our internal messaging operations, you may contact us at the same email address to request information, correction, or deletion as applicable under Brazilian LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados).

8. Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to an internal automation system, including restricting access to authorized staff and protecting OAuth credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we work to reduce risk and respond to issues promptly.

9. Children’s privacy

The Application is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published. The current version will always be available at https://espacomocambo.com.br/n8n-privacy.
